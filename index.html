A medical second opinion is an evaluation of your medical condition, diagnosis, and treatment plan by a specialist who was not involved in your initial care. It provides an independent perspective that can confirm your current diagnosis, suggest alternative treatments, or identify issues that may have been overlooked.

Most second opinions are delivered within 24-48 hours after we receive all your medical records and documentation. For complex cases requiring multiple specialist reviews, it may take up to 5 business days. We also offer expedited services for urgent cases.

You should submit all relevant medical records including: your diagnosis and pathology reports, imaging studies (X-rays, CT scans, MRIs), laboratory test results, current medications list, treatment history, and physician notes. Our team will guide you through the process and help identify any additional documents needed.

All our specialists are board-certified physicians from leading medical institutions including Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, Johns Hopkins, Stanford Health, and other top academic medical centers. They have extensive experience in their respective fields and are carefully vetted to ensure the highest quality of care.

Absolutely. We are fully HIPAA compliant and use enterprise-grade encryption to protect all patient data. Your medical records are only shared with the specialist reviewing your case and are never sold or shared with third parties. We take your privacy extremely seriously.

Our pricing varies depending on the complexity of your case and the specialty required. Basic second opinions start at $299, while more complex cases involving multiple specialists may cost more. We provide transparent pricing upfront, and some insurance plans may cover part or all of the cost. Contact us for a personalized quote.

Yes, we offer optional video consultations with your reviewing specialist. This allows you to ask questions, discuss the findings in detail, and get personalized recommendations. Consultations can be scheduled after you receive your written report and are available for an additional fee.

If our specialist's opinion differs from your original diagnosis, we encourage you to discuss both opinions with your primary care physician. The goal is to give you more information to make informed decisions. Our report will clearly explain any differences and provide recommendations for next steps.

Yes, we serve patients from over 40 countries worldwide. Our platform is designed to work seamlessly across borders. Medical records can be submitted in various languages, and our reports are provided in English with translation services available upon request.

We offer second opinions across all major medical specialties including Oncology, Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pathology, Radiology, Pediatrics, Ophthalmology, ENT, General Surgery, Internal Medicine, Pulmonology, and many more. If you don't see your specialty listed, please contact us.
